Contingency Scorecard

Contingency Adjustment Score

From Undefined cost: 50% of Subtotal Undefined % (rounded to the units)

Site

Score 0%

All needed site information is included in estimate. Soil conditions, underground & above ground obstructions and site conditions are known and included in scope/estimate. No uncertainties exist.

Score 2%

Site visit has been made. Visible conditions and interferences have been observed. Major site conditions, scope and issues are known.

Score 3%

Site scope options are known but may not be final. Major site conditions are assumed.

Score 5%

URC has not completed any significant construction at this site. Site conditions are unknown or site has not been chosen.

%

Resources (Construction, Engineering, & Business)

Score 2%

All required construction, engineering, and business resources are available and experienced with URC, the site, and the process. Construction and sourcing plans exist.

Score 3%

Most project team resources are available. Construction method is known. Wages and productivity based on published data. Availability of skilled construction labor is assumed. Sourcing plan is mostly defined.

Score 4%

Some gap(s) exist in project team resources. Construction method is assumed. Wage rates, productivity and availability of skilled construction labor are assumed. Sourcing plan is not defined.

Score 7%

Significant gap(s) exist in key project team resources. Majority of construction, engineering, and business resources available have little experience with either URC, the site, or the process.

%

Schedule (Excl. Acceleration)

Score 0%

Timing is not an issue.

Score 2%

Minor time constraints exist along with some doubt about capability to achieve schedule

Score 3%

Potential delays, changes in schedule requirements, or teams ability to meet existing schedule could result in schedule change of more than 10% but less than 20%.

Score 5%

Potential change in schedule requirements or team's ability to meet existing schedule could result in schedule change of 20% or more.

%

Pricing

Score 2%

Based on quotes, purchase orders and recent experience. Escalation and currency fluctuation impact is negligible. No import duties or taxes.

Score 4%

Based on details with some allowances. Pricing is from URC experience or database. Escalation and currency fluctuation may be a factor. Import duties/taxes are well known.

Score 3%

Based on allowances and factored cost data. Variation in escalation, currency, import duties/taxes may affect the project cost by 10% or more. Project includes re-use and modification of 30% to 50% of equipment.

Score 4%

Based on allowances and/or escalation. Variation in escalation, currency, import duties/taxes may affect the project cost by 10% or more. Project includes re-use & modification of more than 50% of equipment.
